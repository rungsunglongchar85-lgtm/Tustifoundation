DDU-GKY

About us

Tusti Foundation, with the Ministry of Rural Development and the Government of Nagaland, offers a fully sponsored, government-certified front-office course for rural youth.

Creating careers

Our trainees learn front-office skills, soft skills, English and IT, then practise on the job. Our alumni now work with hotels, airlines and hospitality companies.
