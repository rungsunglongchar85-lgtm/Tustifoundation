FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to pay anything?

No. The course is fully sponsored with no fees and no hidden charges. Uniforms and training materials are free.

Will I need to find a place to stay?

The training is residential and free of cost.

Who can apply?

Rural youth aged 18 to 35 who have passed 10th or higher.

Do I get a job after the course?

You get placement support, and On-the-Job Training gives you real work experience. Our recruiters include hotels, airlines and hospitality brands.

Which documents do I need?

Aadhaar, PAN, indigenous and ST certificates, qualification marksheet, 6 photos, PRC, and one of the listed ration, MGNREGA, SHG or RSBY cards. See the Documents page.

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY), Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, implemented with the Government of Nagaland by Tusti Foundation.
